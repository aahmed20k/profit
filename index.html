How to use this (30 seconds)
  1. Pick a view from the buttons (ranked profit, revenue vs profit, monthly trend, and more).
  2. Hover or tap any bar or point to see exact numbers.
  3. Change assumptions (royalty, nursing cost, months, therapy) to see "what if."
  4. "Kept" means what you keep after paying for the drug, the royalty and the nursing cost you enter. Staff, rent and owner pay are not taken out.

Where these numbers come from

January–November 2026 comes from Vital Care's profitability report (the updated version of the Nov 24 PDF): billed, drug cost, payments and open balance for every claim, which matches CareTend within 1%. October–December 2025 comes from your tracker. Drug names come from the invoice report, or your tracker for the same patient. Royalty defaults are the rates in your tracker. Nursing and delivery are only what the tracker recorded, so use the nursing slider for the rest. Overhead is from the tracker's expense lines. Patients are counted by ID only; where the tracker used two or three IDs for one patient, they are combined.
