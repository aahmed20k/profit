How to use this (30 seconds)
  1. Pick a view from the buttons (ranked profit, revenue vs profit, monthly trend, and more).
  2. Hover or tap any bar or point to see exact numbers.
  3. Change assumptions (royalty, nursing cost, months, therapy) to see "what if."
  4. "Kept" means what you keep after paying for the drug, the royalty and the nursing cost you enter. Staff, rent and owner pay are not taken out.

Where these numbers come from

Drug cost and expected payment are from the Vital Care profitability report (649 claim lines), matched to drug names from the invoice report (647 matched). Royalty is applied at the rate you choose for specialty and immune globulin lines and 19% for everything else. Premeds and supplies are excluded. "Kept" excludes staff, rent and owner pay. The 3-year paths are a simple model, not a forecast. Confirm with your CPA before making loan or hiring decisions.
